About Us

Welcome to KonkanCrest, where the majestic crest of the Sahyadri hills gently rolls down into the azure Arabian Sea. We curate soulful coastal experiences, eco-friendly boutique stays, and immersive cultural getaways across the pristine Konkan belt—from Alibaug and Dapoli to Ratnagiri and Sindhudurg.


Whether you are looking for a secluded palm-fringed villa, an authentic Malvani culinary trail, or an adventure through ancient sea forts, KonkanCrest brings you the very best of coastal heritage with modern comfort.
